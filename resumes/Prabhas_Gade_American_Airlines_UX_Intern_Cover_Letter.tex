Dear American Airlines UX Recruiting Team,

I am applying for the UX Intern role on the Employee Experience Design System team (Req 87871). I am a Computer Science major with a Visual Arts minor at the University of Texas at Dallas (expected May 2028, GPA 3.5), based in the DFW area. I care less about designing a single screen and more about the architecture behind great interfaces---components, patterns, and documentation that make every product feel coherent.

UX is where my two degrees meet. In Visual Arts I learned visual hierarchy, brand identity, and craft; in Computer Science I learned how those decisions have to survive real systems, accessibility constraints, and engineering handoff. That combination is why Design Systems feel like home to me. I want to spend an internship building foundational Figma components---with Auto Layout, Variants, Tokens, and Component Properties---that scale across American's product ecosystem, not throwaway mocks that die in a file.

At ThirdSpaceDigital, I owned UX end-to-end for a community platform. I ran user research and usability testing with early members, did prototyping in Figma, and shaped a reusable design system so article, event, issue, and archive surfaces stayed consistent on web and mobile (including iOS/Android patterns). The product reached 300 users in its first week and held roughly 55 daily visits. I wrote usage guidelines so others could implement components correctly---the same bridge between design and code this role asks for.

Tactile is the project that shows how obsessed I am with Figma craft. I built a gesture-driven Figma plugin that streams roughly 60 FPS hand tracking into design actions, then rebuilt the interaction model four times until false triggers stopped breaking the workflow. It taught me to standardize repeating patterns, document edge cases, and treat designer tools with the same seriousness as customer-facing UI.

At Vinson Process Controls, I partnered in an Agile team to define user stories and ship a monitoring interface that improved operator efficiency by 1200\%. Collecting stakeholder feedback and turning critiques into clearer interaction patterns is work I already enjoy---and it maps directly to supporting product managers and designers on your Portfolio and Product teams.

I would be proud to help American's Design System stay scalable, accessible, and high-fidelity for the people who keep the largest airline in the world running. My portfolio is at studiorain.xyz. Thank you for your time---I would love the chance to contribute in Fort Worth.
