I am looking for a team where problem solving means envisioning a better way to complete a task---higher efficiency, better user experience, and technology applied to real business problems with measurable results. I would be glad to bring that mindset back into the Emerson ecosystem, this time building the development tools and diagnostics that help engineers investigate and improve the platforms customers rely on.
